## Lamp in the Window

Every night Marta lit one lamp and set it where the sea could see it. Her husband called it superstition. She called it manners.

## A Knock at Midnight

Three soft knocks, then two. It was the signal, though the voice that followed was not his.

## What the Tide Returned

In the morning she found a crate on the sand, and inside it, to her surprise, only books.
