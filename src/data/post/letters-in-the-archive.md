This is sample text. Historical fiction begins, for me, in the archive: a municipal register, a ship's manifest, a letter whose ink has gone the color of tea.

## The detail that changes everything

A single line, a grocer's debt or a misspelled surname, can unlock a scene. Facts give the story its walls; invention furnishes the rooms.

## What I leave out

Research is a lake and the novel is a cup of water. Most of what I learn never reaches the page, and it still shapes every sentence.
