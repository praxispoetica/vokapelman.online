This is sample text. I write in the early morning, before the day has opinions. Tea first, then one hour with no internet.

## Drafting by hand

The first draft of every chapter is longhand. The typewriter comes later, when the voice is already found.

## Finishing

A novel is finished one unglamorous page at a time. The only trick is showing up the next day.
